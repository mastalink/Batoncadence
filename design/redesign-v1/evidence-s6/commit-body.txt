Schedules in plain words, no raw 403s, settings without jargon (redesign slice 6)

Schedules: src/mco/schedules_plain.py is the one implementation. `bitcadence
schedule` / `schedule add|on|off` and the console Schedules page (/api/schedules,
/preview, /{id}/enabled) call it. "Every weekday at 2 AM" in, "Every weekday at
2:00 AM" out; bad words get the CLI.md sentence and nothing is saved. New entries
are appended to ~/.mco/schedules.yaml as text (comments survive), the time zone is
detected, and the file is read back before it is written. Old `mco schedule`
commands are unchanged.

Approvals: a missing approver right is one sentence plus the one-key "Fix it?
[Y/n]" in `bitcadence approve` and `bitcadence fix` (src/mco/approver.py). The
repair is terminal-only, local-database-only and edits only this computer's own
account; no API route can grant it. The console turns an HTTP 403 into words and an
Approvals card. tests/conftest.py now keeps every test off the real registry.

Settings: `bitcadence settings` and the console show plain labels; addresses and
the (masked) sign-in appear only under --show-advanced / "Show advanced".
